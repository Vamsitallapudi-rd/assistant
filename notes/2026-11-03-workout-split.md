# Workout split (what I try to follow)

## Week at a glance
| Day | Focus | Non-negotiables (🔒) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Upper A (push/pull + power) | Plyo push-up, DB incline press, BB row, push press, face pull, farmer carry |
| Tue | Lower A (squat focus) | Box jump, back squat, RDL/ham curl, Bulgarian split squat, seated calf, Spanish squat hold |
| Wed | Engine (treadmill) | VO₂ intervals, Zone 2 40 min, side plank |
| Thu | Upper B (pull-up + rotation) | Landmine rotational press, pull-up/pulldown, landmine press, chest-supported row, flat DB bench, lateral raise |
| Fri | Lower B (hinge focus) | Broad/jump squat, KB swing, trap bar DL, reverse lunge, ham curl, ab wheel, mid-thigh pull |
| Sat | Run + agility (treadmill) | Run progression, farmer carry, single-leg calf iso, mobility |
| Sun | Mobility + movement | 90/90, deep squat, world's greatest stretch, couch stretch, dead hang 5 min |

**Legend:** 🔒 = always do, first, in order · ⭐ = do on a normal day · unmarked = extras if time/energy.

## Training rules
**Priority**
- Very short day (under 40 min): 🔒 power + 🔒 main strength only. Never skip main lifts for accessories.
- Short on time for isometrics: do the 🔒 ones first, then Spanish squat → mid-thigh pull → long-lever hamstring bridge → side plank + 90/90 → mid-range pull-up hold.

**Effort**
| Block | How hard | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Never to failure. Stop when speed drops. | 60–90s |
| Main strength | RIR 1–2 (1–2 good reps left) | 2–3 min |
| Accessories | Controlled, RIR 1–2 | 60–90s |
| Core | Stop when position breaks | 60–90s |
| Isolation/AMRAP finishers | Technical failure (not sloppy) | 45–60s |
| Carries | Stop when posture or grip slips | – |
| Isometrics | RPE 6–7 (~70%), steady breathing. Mid-thigh pull: 5s at ~80% | 30–45s |

**Progression**
- Lifts: hit the top of the rep range at the right RIR → add weight next session.
- Isometrics: add 5–10s or a bit of load. Once 45s feels easy, make it harder instead of longer.
- Zone 2: raise incline/speed only while HR stays under 145.
- Intervals: add 0.5 km/h or 1% incline once all 6 rounds feel the same.
